Our Story

Since 1995, Casa del Sol has been serving authentic Mexican cuisine made with love and tradition. Our recipes have been passed down through generations, bringing the true flavors of Mexico to your table.

We use only the freshest ingredients, traditional cooking methods, and authentic spices to create dishes that transport you straight to the heart of Mexico.

  • Fresh, locally-sourced ingredients
  • Traditional family recipes
  • Warm, family-friendly atmosphere
